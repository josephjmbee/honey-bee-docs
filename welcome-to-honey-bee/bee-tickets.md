# 🎫 Bee Tickets

***

#### 1. Bee Tickets & Economy

* **Bee Tickets:** The premium currency used within the Honey Bee ecosystem. <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/zvmG3v3x/HB-Ticket.png" alt="" data-size="line">
* **Earning Tickets:** Participate in server activities, vote for the bot on Top.gg, and complete expeditions to earn tickets. <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/zvmG3v3x/HB-Ticket.png" alt="" data-size="line">

***

#### 2. The Ticket Shop (`/ticket shop`)

* **Command:** `/ticket shop` _(or `/ticket buy`)_
* **Description:** Open the ticket bazaar to browse and acquire special items, boosters, and rare companion bees.
* **Available Stock:** Includes utility cards like HP Restorations, XP Boosters, Nectar Packs, Shields, and special seasonal bees.

<figure><img src="https://i.postimg.cc/LsPjccP3/tkshopbee.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 3. Leaderboards & Competition

* **Global Rankings:** Compete against other servers and users to see who rules the honey empire.
* **Climbing the Ranks:** Accumulate nectar, level up your companion bees, and maintain active hives to push your standing higher on the leaderboards.
