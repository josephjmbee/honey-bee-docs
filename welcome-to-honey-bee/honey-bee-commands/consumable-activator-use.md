# 🧪 Consumable Activator (/use)

| **Command Name** | **Usage**            | **Variables**                                                                                                                                                        | **Description**                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Use              | `/use item:<choice>` | `item` (Required choices: `hp_card` \[HP Restoration Card], `xp_card` \[XP Booster Card], `nectar_pack` \[Meadow Nectar Pack], `amber_shield` \[Royal Amber Shield]) | Consumes an item from your satchel to restore companion health, boost XP, store raw nectar, or freeze HP decay. |
