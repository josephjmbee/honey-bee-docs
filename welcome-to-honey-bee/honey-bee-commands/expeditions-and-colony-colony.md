# 🧭 Expeditions & Colony (/colony)

| **Usage**                            | **Subcommand** | **Variables**                                                                                                           | **Description**                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/colony dispatch duration:<choice>` | Dispatch       | `duration` (Required choices: `1h` \[Low Risk], `2h` \[Low Risk], `3h` \[Moderate], `6h` \[Moderate], `12h` \[Extreme]) | Sends your companion bee on a timed Nectar Expedition. Generates a custom 850x420 vintage map canvas displaying randomized route nodes, risks, and timers. |
| `/colony claim`                      | Claim          | None                                                                                                                    | Collects your accrued expedition loot once the timer completes. Rewards raw server nectar, companion XP, and bonus tickets.                                |
