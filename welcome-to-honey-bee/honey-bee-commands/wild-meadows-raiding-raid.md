# ⚔️ Wild Meadows Raiding (/raid)

| **Usage**                      | **Subcommand** | **Variables**                                                                                                     | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/raid wild difficulty:<tier>` | Wild           | `difficulty` (Required choices: `easy` \[Dandelion Clearing], `medium` \[Thorn Thicket], `hard` \[Hornet Hollow]) | Embarks on a solo wild expedition with dynamic weather rolls (e.g., _Monsoon Rains_, _Scorching Heatwave_). Success yields honey, utility cards, and random Lifespan Extensions (ranging from +30 mins to +3 days). |
