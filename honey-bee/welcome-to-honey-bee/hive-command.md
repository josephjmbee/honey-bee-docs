# 🍯 Hive Command

***

#### 1. <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/ZK301GrD/HB-Hive.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Check Vault & Hive Status

* **Command:** `/hive`
* **Description:** Displays your current hive overview, including stored honey, pantry food crates, and max capacity.
* **Arguments:**
  * `user:{@xyz}` — Optional argument to see your friend or other player's hive.

<figure><img src="https://i.postimg.cc/43P4Yptt/hiveprofile.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 2. Hive Resources & Pantry

* **Food Crates:** Used by the `/feed` to nourish your companion bee and keep it healthy.
* **Storage Capacity:** Tracks your maximum limit for storing honey. You can expand your hive's capacity using upgrades from the `/ticket shop`!
