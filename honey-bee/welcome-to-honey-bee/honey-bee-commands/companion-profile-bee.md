# 🐝 Companion Profile (/bee)

| **Command Name** | **Usage**     | **Variables**                                                | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bee              | `/bee [user]` | `user` (Optional user mention to inspect another user's bee) | Displays a comprehensive 900x640 canvas dossier tracking companion health brackets, XP progress, birthtime age, 7-day lifespan countdowns, economy metrics, and unlocked badges. |
