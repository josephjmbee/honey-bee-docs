# 🏡 Hive Architecture & Reserves (/hive)

| **Command Name** | **Usage**      | **Variables**                                                      | **Description**                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Hive             | `/hive [user]` | `user` (Optional user mention to view another beekeeper's profile) | Renders a detailed 900x620 canvas dossier showing your architecture tier (_Standard Meadow_ up to _Celestial Citadel_), honey vault storage bars, hive health/shields, food stores, local nectar, and inventory pills. |
