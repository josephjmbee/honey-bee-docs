# 📦 Inventory System

***

#### 1. What is Your Inventory?

Your inventory holds all the special items you purchase from the **`/ticket shop`** ,**`/honey shop`** or earn through events and expeditions. You can view your satchel contents anytime to see what tools you have available.

* **Command:** `/inventory`
* **Description:** track all your collected utility items, booster cards, and protective gear in your personal satche.

<figure><img src="https://i.postimg.cc/HWq8gc6d/inventory.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 2. Stored Items & Their Uses

* 🧪 **HP Restoration Card:** Restores **10–40 HP** to your active companion bee when health is running low.
* ⚡ **XP Booster Card:** Grants **100–300 XP** instantly to speed up your bee's leveling progress.
* 🌸 **Meadow Nectar Pack:** Yields **1k–3k Nectar** directly for your hive.
* 🛡️ **Royal Amber Shield:** Freezes your bee's status decay for **12 hours** to keep them safe.

<figure><img src="https://i.postimg.cc/HWq8gc6d/inventory.webp" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

#### 3. How to Use Items

* **Command:** `/use`
* **Description:** Select an item from your satchel dropdown menu to activate it instantly and support your active companion bee or hive.
* **Arguments:**
  * `(item)` — Required argument to select which item you want to activate from your inventory.
