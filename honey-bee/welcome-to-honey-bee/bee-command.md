## **Companion (Bee) Info**

## ⏳ Companion Bee Lifespan & Lifecycle

When you adopt a companion bee in Honey Bee, your new friend comes with a natural 7-day lifespan. This mechanic keeps the gameplay active, strategic, and rewarding!

#### How the Lifespan Works:

* **Active Duration:** Every newly adopted companion bee stays active and healthy for 7 full days (measured from the exact timestamp of adoption).
* **Countdown Tracking:** You can check your companion's current health, level, and active status anytime using the `/bee` command.
* **Expiration:** Once the 7-day lifespan is reached, your bee completes its journey. Don't worry you can easily adopt or purchase a new companion from the `/ticket shop`!

#### 💖 Can You Extend It?

* Yes! If you want your companion bee to stay by your side longer, you can use rare utility items like the **`Magic Miracle`** from the ticket shop, which adds 3 extra days to your bee's active lifespan.

#### 2. <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/pV388Wst/HB-Honey-Pot.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Feed Your Companion (Bee)

{% hint style="info" %}
Keep your companion bee healthy, strong, and full of energy by feeding it regularly from your hive pantry!
{% endhint %}

* **Command:** `/feed {amount 1 to 6}`
* **Description:** Consumes food crates from your hive pantry to nourish your active companion bee, preventing it from getting tired, weak, or sick.
* **Requirements:**
  * You must have an active companion (bee) or adopt it via (`/ticket shop` and `/ticket buy`).
  * Your hive pantry must have available food crates (which you can stock up on using `/ticket buy [list : Bee Food]`)!
  * **Cost:** `1 Bee Ticket` <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/zvmG3v3x/HB-Ticket.png" alt="" data-size="line">&#x20;
  * **Quantity:** `30 Food`&#x20;
* **Benefits:**
  * **Health Restoration:**
    * Each food crate consumed restores a random 3 to 6 HP, capped at a maximum of `100 HP`
  * **Lifespan Extension:**
    * Each crate grants a base `5 to 10 minutes` of extra lifespan.
  * **Rarity Multipliers:**
    * Your lifespan boost is scaled up by your bee's rarity tier (Common, Rare, etc.)
  * **Golden Winged Evolution Bonus:**
    * If your companion has evolved into the Golden Winged state, you get an `additional +25% bonus` boost on your `lifespan extension`!

#### 3. <img src="https://i.postimg.cc/T1bjLv9j/HB-Vault.png" alt="" data-size="line"> Using Inventory Items

{% hint style="info" %}
Got special items stored in your satchel? Use them to boost your bee's stats, restore health, or protect your progress!
{% endhint %}

* **Command:** `/use`
* **Description:** Activates a utility item from your satchel to support your active companion bee or hive.
* **Arguments:**
  * `(item)` — Required argument to select which item you want to activate from your inventory.

{% hint style="info" %}
🧪 Available Satchel Items & Effects

* 🧪 HP Restoration Card: Restores 10–40 HP to your active companion bee to keep it healthy.
* ⚡ XP Booster Card: Grants 100–300 XP to fast-track your bee's leveling progress.
* 🌸 Meadow Nectar Pack: Instantly yields 1k–3k Nectar for your hive.
* 🛡️ Royal Amber Shield: Freezes your bee's status decay for 12 hours.
{% endhint %}
